HabitCal leaderboard: three options (T-015)

Each option is the popover at its real 260 pt width, in light and dark mode. The trophy button sits in the Habits header row, next to + and Settings (T-014), and opens the leaderboard as its own screen with Back, like Settings. Sample data: today is Thursday, October 29, 2026, with five habits. Pick one, or mix parts of them, and it gets written into the README's Answer and then a spec.

Entry point: the Habits header row

The trophy goes between + and Settings. Highlighted here in blue.

Habits
Habits

1 · Streak Board

Ranked by current streak. The bar is the current streak as a share of that habit's best, and it turns to flame colors when the current streak is the best. Simple, and it uses numbers the habit rows already show.

2 · Consistency

Ranked by the share of due days done in the chosen range, so a habit due twice a week competes fairly with a daily one. The overall score at the top covers every habit. Click the range buttons.

3 · This Week

A weekly scorecard: one headline number, then each habit's seven days (done, missed, not due, or still to come), ranked by how many it has done. A nudge appears when a habit is within two days of its best streak.
